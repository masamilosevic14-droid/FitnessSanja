About me

I'm a certified fitness coach with over ten years of experience working with clients in Serbia and the United Arab Emirates. My goal is simple: to help you feel stronger, healthier and more confident in your body, with training that is safe, effective and designed around you.

I've worked as a trainer in gyms in Belgrade and Abu Dhabi, and today I coach clients independently.
