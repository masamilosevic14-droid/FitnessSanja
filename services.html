Training designed around you

In one-on-one training the whole session is about you: your goal, your fitness level and your pace. There's no one-size-fits-all program. Everything we do is chosen for what your body needs right now.

We start with a physical assessment and a conversation about what you want to achieve. It might be losing weight, toning up, getting stronger or simply feeling better in your body. From there, I design a safe, science-based training program built around your needs.

During our sessions I guide you through every exercise and help you with technique, so you train correctly and with confidence. I track your progress along the way and adjust your program as you improve. When you reach a goal, we set the next one.

BODYPUMP Group Training

BODYPUMP is a Les Mills group workout that uses a barbell and weights, set to music. You work through all the major muscle groups one track at a time, using light to moderate weights and lots of repetitions. You build strength and endurance, and the energy of the group keeps you going all the way to the last track.

I'm a certified Les Mills BODYPUMP instructor, Level 2. That means I've been trained and assessed against Les Mills' international standards, so every class is structured, safe and led with attention to technique.

In class I show you each movement, give clear cues and help you pick the right weight for your level. You set your own load, so beginners and experienced participants can train side by side. You'll get stronger and more confident with every class.

Staying active through pregnancy, and returning to training after birth

Pregnancy and the months after giving birth bring big changes to your body. The right kind of movement can help you feel stronger, more energised and more at ease in your body. The key is to train in a way that is safe and suited to the stage you're in.

I've completed Pre and Post Natal Fitness training with Inspire Fitness Academy, a program recognised by REPs UAE, ACSM and ACE. With that training and more than ten years of experience working with clients, I help women stay active during pregnancy and return to training gradually after birth.

We start with a conversation about how you feel, where you are in your pregnancy or recovery, and what you'd like to achieve. From there I put together a program that fits your current stage and energy level. I guide you through every exercise, keep an eye on how your body responds, and adjust the plan as you progress.
